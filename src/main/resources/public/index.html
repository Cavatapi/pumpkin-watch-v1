WELCOME TO THE NIGHT SHIFT

A little courage after dark.

Play alone or with up to seven friends as living pumpkins and scarecrows. Protect your sleeping farmer through five waves, then defeat the Skeleton King.

  1. Gather at dusk. Single player starts immediately; multiplayer begins once everyone is ready. You have 60 seconds to plant. In single player, use Start the wave when you are ready.
  2. Grow your garden. Walk to a plot and choose a defense. Collect glowing moonseeds to fill the shared bank. Moonflowers grow extra seeds. Skeletons approach the central farmhouse from all sides. Cannons face outward and spray nine shots across a semicircle; select a plot to preview its coverage.
  3. Keep it standing. Defenses fire automatically. Hold E or the repair button near damaged defenses. Repairs cost time, not seeds. Upgrade for more damage and health.
  4. Find your combo. Each 90-second wave is followed by cleanup, then 20 seconds to vote on a charm. Matching charms stack. Tied votes are decided randomly.
  5. Make it to morning. Hits to the house reduce the farmer's sleep. At 0%, the farmer wakes and the run ends. The King reaching the house wakes the farmer immediately. Clear wave five and defeat its King to win.

Team score: 100 per skeleton + 500 per wave + 2,000 bonus for the King + 100 per remaining sleep percentage on victory. Your upgrades reset each run.

WASD / arrows to move. Tap or click ground to walk. Click a plot to select it. Hold E to repair. Hold right mouse and drag to pan the map; press Space to recenter and follow your character. On touch screens use Pan mode and the recenter button. The plant shop and charm collection are inside the game window. Skeletons stop to attack you when close—distract them, but keep moving! If you become a ghost, you return in five seconds.
